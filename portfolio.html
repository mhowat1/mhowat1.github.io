CC02 - Creating custom visualizations

Coffee, Tea, and Cocoa CPI Weight

This chart shows the relative weights of coffee, tea, and cocoa in the Consumer Price Index (CPI) over time. The data is sourced from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics and reflects changes in consumer spending patterns for these beverages.

Nuclear Power in Great Britain

This chart shows the output of nuclear power in Great Britain over time. The data is sourced from the IEA via ECO-API.
