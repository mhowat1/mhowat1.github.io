About

I'm an MPA Candidate in Data Science for Public Policy at the London School of Economics and a Research Assistant at the Grantham Research Institute on Climate Change and the Environment, where I work on agri-food systems transitions. Before LSE, I spent four years at Epic Systems implementing population health software and supporting research with the Cosmos database.

I'm currently based in London. You can reach me at m.l.howat@lse.ac.uk.
